Kitchen renovation costs in Mandurah

In short: We don't publish our own prices. A national cost guide suggests budgeting about A$30,000–50,000 for a basic full kitchen renovation, but a local renovator has to quote your kitchen.

Use published ranges only as a starting point, and add a contingency.

Last updated: 24 November 2026

We don't set or advertise our own prices. The figures below are from a published national guide, with a link. They are not quotes for Mandurah. A local renovator has to price your kitchen.

Published cost ranges

  • hipages' kitchen renovation cost guide (updated January 2026) suggests budgeting around A$30,000–50,000 for a basic full kitchen renovation, based on national averages.
  • It says a kitchen can be done for about A$30,000 if the doors, walls, sink and other plumbing stay where they are and materials are affordable, and that bigger spaces or high-end products can add another A$10,000–20,000.
  • It recommends adding 15–20% to the estimate for contingency.

What usually changes the quote

Layout changes

Moving the sink, cooktop or dishwasher means moving plumbing, gas or electrics. Removing walls may need a builder, engineering and approval.

Cabinetry and benchtops

Flat-pack, semi-custom or custom joinery, and benchtop materials such as laminate, engineered stone alternatives, timber or natural stone.

Appliances

Whether you supply your own appliances or the renovator includes them.

The house

The older foreshore and Mandurah city streets mix units and earlier houses, Halls Head, Dudley Park and Erskine have family homes and canal-side streets, and Meadow Springs, Greenfields, Lakelands and Madora Bay have large newer estates. Older homes can hide surprises such as old wiring, uneven floors or asbestos-containing materials, which add cost.

Flooring, lighting and finishes

Splashbacks, flooring, lighting, painting and making good the surrounding rooms.

About the budget field on our form

The budget bands are for you to show a range if you have one. They are not a price list.
